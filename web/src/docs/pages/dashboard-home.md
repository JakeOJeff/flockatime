# Home

The Home page is your coding at a glance: editor time from Hackatime beside what snapshot-agent captured, the last two weeks day by day, and where the time went.

Two colours carry the source everywhere: **red** is Hackatime, **blue** is snapshot-agent.

## Date range

The pills at the top right pick the range for the tiles, projects and languages: **Today**, **7 days**, **30 days** or **All time** (the default). The headline sentence and the 14-day chart always describe now, whatever the range.

## Tiles

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Editor time | Hackatime editor time in the range, and how many projects it covers |
| Captured | Time snapshot-agent saw your files changing, summed over every repo |
| Lines written | Lines added in the range, with lines removed |
| Health | Whether the snapshot history chain is intact; saves, builds and tests; and how many agents are behind the newest version |

## Last 14 days

Hackatime and snapshot-agent time per day, side by side. Hover a day for exact numbers. The total counts each day once, at whichever source saw more of it.

## Projects

Every project either source knows, by Hackatime time: a red bar and time for Hackatime, a blue bar and time for snapshot-agent. **untracked** means snapshot-agent has never seen that repo. Hackatime counts reading time too, so it usually runs higher. **See all** opens the [projects page](#/docs/dashboard-projects).

## Languages

Share of Hackatime time by language: the top five, then the rest folded into **N more**. Below it, your main editor and OS once the Hackatime plugin reports them.

If Hackatime cannot be read, its tiles and panels say why. See [Hackatime](#/docs/hackatime).
